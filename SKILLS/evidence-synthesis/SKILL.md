# Evidence Synthesis

## Overview

Turn scattered sources into a usable `evidence-matrix.md` with confidence and implication tracking.

## Workflow

1. Gather the relevant sources.
2. Record the date and why each source matters.
3. Extract the claim made by the source.
4. Translate each claim into an implication for the current initiative.
5. Record the remaining open question or verification gap.

## Quality Rules

- Prefer primary sources and recent information.
- Mark weak or indirect evidence clearly.
- Separate evidence from your interpretation.
