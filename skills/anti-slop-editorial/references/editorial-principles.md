# Editorial Principles

Use this reference when the draft needs a deeper pass than the main skill body should carry.

## People-First Standard

Before style, make the piece answer:

1. What happened?
2. Why does it matter?
3. Who is affected?
4. What changed?
5. What evidence supports the claim?
6. What should the reader understand, decide, or do next?

## Lexical Register

Check whether the draft:

- sounds natural for the intended reader
- uses jargon because it helps, not because it signals status
- stays concrete enough to be believed
- matches the genre instead of drifting into brand copy

## Practitioner Vocabulary

Prefer words a teammate could say in a review, memo, or postmortem:

- rollout
- failure mode
- migration
- threshold
- latency
- reviewer
- queue
- handoff
- validation

## Diagnostic Questions

- Is the main claim supported or only decorated?
- Did the writer describe a mechanism or only a benefit?
- Would this sentence sound natural in a real team discussion?
- Is the vocabulary improving comprehension or just raising the register?
